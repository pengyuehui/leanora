Leanora Fitness 1.4 (8). No account, login, subscription or purchase is required. The interface and original guides are in Chinese. All new content is bundled and works offline. The app retains its existing optional Apple Health integration and privacy answers; no new Health permissions, network content downloads or data collection are introduced.

New review flows:
1. Today > 日历与周回顾 (Calendar and Weekly Recap), also available in Trends. Browse previous/next weeks, select a date, and open that day's food diary or a source workout. Only local business records are aggregated. Completed and abandoned workouts are distinguishable; abandoned workouts are not counted as completed. External Health sources remain on the existing source page.
2. Training > 动作库与收藏 (Exercise Library) > any exercise > 动作历史与进步 (Exercise History). After completing working sets, view per-session metrics, compare the latest two and open the source workout. Only completed working sets in completed workouts with the same exercise ID and measurement type qualify. Bodyweight, assisted repetitions, external load, duration and cardio metrics remain separate. There is no 1RM estimate or automatic effectiveness rating. An empty state appears until qualifying records exist.
3. Diary > 复制整日饮食 (Copy Day), or Calendar > selected day > copy. Select source/target dates and meal categories, preview then explicitly confirm. A day must contain records to copy, and target day must differ. Existing target records are preserved. Each copy retains portions, notes and nutrient snapshots as independent entries. Unknown nutrient values remain unknown. Preview and cancel create no records or Health samples. A failed commit can retry the same batch without duplicate Health intents. Maximum 200 entries per copy.
4. Training Recording Guides and Nutrition Recording Guides each contain 12 original offline Chinese articles, including 4 new review guides in each collection. Food-combination examples and training programs remain editable recording examples, not individualized prescriptions.

Existing features:
Free workouts, templates, weekly schedules, previous set performance, repeat from history, rest/timed exercise recovery, 882 exercises with 1,765 offline static guidance images; a 500-food sourced catalog (209 Chinese names), custom foods, portions, quick calories and editable food combinations; weight and body-fat records; JSON/CSV export and previewed backup restore. JSON format 2 requires version 1.3+ to restore; format 1 is still readable. Editing or deleting original records recomputes summaries.

Apple Health: My > Apple Health lets the user choose access and directions. Existing known nutrient components, completed workouts and body records sync through the component queue. Historical export requires a separate confirmation. Favorites, reading guides, analytics and previewing copies do not create samples. Health data is not sent to the developer. Manual recording works without Health access.

Missing dates are not treated as zero intake. A day whose recorded nutrient fields are populated does not imply the full day was logged. Exercise energy is not automatically added to food goals. Source attribution, licenses and privacy controls remain available in the app.
