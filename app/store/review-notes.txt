Leanora Fitness is an offline native iPhone workout, food and body journal. No sign-in, ads, analytics, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Health access is optional. Production starts with an empty personal journal; App Store screenshots use an isolated test store that is excluded from Release.

Version 1.3 review flow:
1. Finish onboarding; skip Health if desired. Food Diary > Add Breakfast opens food search. Try Chinese or English names, category filters, Favorites, Recent and Custom. Open a food, star it, choose a sourced unit or 50/100/150 g shortcut, inspect the nutrient preview and save. Previous grams are an explicit choice, not an inferred intake.
2. Food Diary > My Meal Combinations > New: name the combination, add foods or quick calories, edit quantities, reorder/remove items, then explicitly save. This does not record intake or write Health samples.
3. Open the saved combination > Record This Combination. Select servings, meal and time; 0.5 scales every item by half. Confirm to save independent diet entries. The entire batch and eligible Health intents commit atomically. Updating/deleting a combination never rewrites historical intake.
4. A nonempty diary meal has Save as Combination, which opens an unsaved draft. Cancel creates no combination. Food-combination examples similarly preview without writes, then open editable drafts on adoption.
5. Diary > Food-combination Examples contains 6 original editable examples; Nutrition Recording Guides contains 8 original offline articles in Chinese. Nutrition is calculated from each sourced food/state/gram amount, with no personalized diet prescription. Extra oil, sugar and other ingredients must be recorded separately.
6. Check daily known totals, missing-nutrient counts and user-entered calorie goals. Empty days are unknown, not zero intake. Quick calories do not infer macros; exercise energy is not automatically added to food goals.
7. My > Data Management: JSON format 2 exports favorites, combinations and business records; it requires 1.3+ to restore. Older format 1 remains readable. CSV exports actual records. Import previews, identity replacement safeguards and Health reconciliation after restore are retained.

Existing training: start a free workout or a template; record actual sets, pause/resume and finish. History supports repeat and completed-working-set template drafts. Existing exercise guidance remains offline: 882 exercises, 1,765 licensed static images, 4 original programs, 9 templates and 8 training guides.

My > Apple Health: choose read/write categories and grant system access. Eligible records sync through the existing component-based queue; status, sources and pending work are inspectable. Historical export needs a separate confirmation. Nutrition templates, favorites and reading content create no Health samples. Health data is not sent to the developer. No new permission categories, network content downloads or data collection in 1.3.

The 500 foods use official USDA FoodData Central SR Legacy 2018 values under CC0; 209 Chinese labels and the new examples/guides are independently authored. This is not a comprehensive Chinese brand/restaurant database. Some names and portions remain English. The app provides no diagnosis, treatment or personalized nutrition advice.
