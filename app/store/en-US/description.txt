Every set. Every meal. A record that stays with you.

Leanora Fitness brings workouts, food portions and body measurements into a personal journal. No account is required, and everyday logging works offline. The interface and original guides are provided in Chinese; some exercise names, food names and source instructions remain in English.

DAILY CALENDAR AND WEEKLY RECAP
Review local workouts, four meal categories and body records together. Browse weeks and open a selected day's diary. Track training days, workout counts, completed sets and food-record coverage. The known-energy average excludes dates without known energy. Having records does not mean a full day was logged. Unknown nutrients stay unknown.

EXERCISE HISTORY
View completed working sets for the same exercise and measurement type. Compare the latest two sessions and open each source workout. External load, bodyweight, assisted repetitions, time and distance use separate metrics. The app does not estimate 1RM or automatically judge training effectiveness.

TRAIN YOUR WAY
Build templates with target sets, repetitions, load and rest. Schedule a week or reschedule one occurrence. Start a free workout, add exercises, record each set, pause and continue. Review previous performance, reuse values for unfinished sets or repeat a completed workout. Turn working sets into an editable template draft before saving.

OFFLINE EXERCISE RESOURCES
Explore 882 exercises and 1,765 bundled guidance images with full-screen viewing. Filter by muscle, equipment, difficulty or type. Favorite regular exercises and browse recently used ones. Four original example programs provide 9 editable templates, with 12 offline recording and review guides. Examples leave personal load and weekly scheduling to you. Images are static photos or illustrations.

KEEP A FOOD DIARY
Log breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks. Search 500 sourced foods with 209 independently prepared Chinese names. Combine name, alias, category, favorite and recent filters. Choose sourced portions, gram shortcuts or explicitly reuse a previous gram amount. Create custom foods or log quick calories. Missing macros are not inferred from calories.

REUSE MEALS OR A DAY
Save editable food combinations and preview fractional or multiple servings before recording. Copy a past meal or select meal categories from a day, preview and confirm independent entries. Portions, notes, nutrient snapshots and unknown values are preserved. Existing target-day records stay in place. Explore 6 original food-combination examples and 12 offline nutrition-recording guides in Chinese.

BODY TRENDS AND APPLE HEALTH
Log weight and body fat manually and review 7-day or 30-day trends. Exercise energy is not automatically added to food goals. Choose system Health access and sync directions for workouts, nutrition and body data. Review available activity summaries, sources, pending work and sync status, or stop automatic sync. Historical export requires separate confirmation. Manual logging works without Health access.

YOUR DATA ON YOUR DEVICE
Export JSON backups and CSV files, preview imports and restore backups. Manage local records, imported Health caches and Health copies written by this app separately. Keep your exported backups. JSON format 2 requires version 1.3 or a later compatible version to restore; format 1 remains readable.

The food catalog focuses on basic foods. Content supports personal recording and does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment or individualized nutrition prescriptions.
