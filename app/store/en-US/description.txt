Every set. Every meal. A record that stays with you.

Leanora Fitness brings training, nutrition and body measurements into one personal journal. Build your own routine, log what you actually lift, record meal portions and review your history. No account is required, and everyday logging works offline.

TRAIN YOUR WAY
Choose exercises, build templates, set target sets, repetitions, load and rest periods, then schedule your training week. Record each set as you go, pause when needed and continue later. History keeps a snapshot of the plan used for each session.

FROM FREE WORKOUTS TO PERSONAL TEMPLATES
Start without preparing a routine, then add exercises as you go. View previous performance beside each set, reuse values for unfinished sets or repeat a workout from history. Turn completed working sets into an editable template draft. Updating the original template always requires an explicit save.

FIND YOUR REGULAR EXERCISES
Favorite exercises and browse recently used ones. Explore 4 original example programs with 9 editable templates and 8 offline guides about recording, timers and statistics. Example programs leave load and weekly scheduling to you. These new programs and guides are provided in Chinese.

KEEP A FOOD DIARY
Log breakfast, lunch, dinner and snacks. Calculate calories, protein, carbohydrates and fat from your portions. Create custom foods, record calories quickly or copy a previous meal. Basic foods include source information, and missing nutrients remain unknown rather than being treated as zero.

REVIEW YOUR RECORDS
Explore 7-day or 30-day views of completed workouts, active time, working-set volume, food energy and body measurements. Log body weight and body fat manually. Exercise energy is not automatically added to your food target.

CONNECT APPLE HEALTH ON YOUR TERMS
Choose access in the system permission screen. Sync workouts, nutrition and body data in the directions you authorize, and read available activity summaries. Review sources, pending items and sync status, or stop automatic sync. Exporting historical records requires a separate confirmation. Manual logging remains available without Health access.

KEEP CONTROL OF YOUR DATA
Your journal is stored on your device. Export JSON backups and CSV files, restore a backup, and manage local records, imported Health caches and Health copies written by this app separately. Keep your exported backups in a safe place.

The food catalog focuses on basic foods and is not a comprehensive restaurant or branded-food database. Leanora does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment or personalized nutrition prescriptions.

OFFLINE EXERCISE GUIDANCE
Explore 882 exercises with 1,765 bundled guidance images. Browse images full screen while choosing exercises or during a workout. Search and filter by equipment, muscle, difficulty or exercise type. Guidance uses static photos or illustrations; some names and instructions remain in English.
